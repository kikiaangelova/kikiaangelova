Eighteen years in training rooms and over a thousand people trained — group dynamics, organizational behaviour, soft skills — for UniCredit Bulbank, Renault Nissan, Schneider Electric and CEZ Bulgaria, across Bulgaria, Europe and the US. Mentoring young people since 2010, before that was a recognised profession in Bulgaria. Sport psychologist since 2026. And, for the last two years, a marketing and digital innovation teacher at SoftUni Buditel.

I'm not a developer. I build with AI because the things I needed — for a skater, for a student, for a training room — didn't exist, and I stopped waiting for someone else to make them. Two of the three apps below started with my daughter asking for something. Everything here is in progress and built in public.

### Where the work comes from

**Training and assessment.** Certified group dynamics trainer and assessor; organizational behaviour trainer since 2006. **Over 1,000 people trained in 18 years.** Topics: communication, decision-making, project management, team building and development, leadership and management, conflict management, negotiation, time management, stress management, and personal and organizational development. Corporate clients include UniCredit Bulbank, Renault Nissan, Schneider Electric and CEZ Bulgaria, delivered across Bulgaria, Europe and the US.

**Mentoring, since 2010** — before mentoring was a recognised profession in Bulgaria. My own benchmark: at least one entrepreneur out of every cohort. Results with teams I mentored:

- **3rd place** — first Defense Tech Hackathon, Munich 2024, with a team of my own students
- **3rd place** — HackUPC 2025, Barcelona
- **HackYeah 2025**, Poland — two 3rd places, one 4th and one 6th

**Sport psychology.** Practice since 2026 — and the reason IceNotes exists.

**Teaching, for the last two years.** Marketing and digital innovation at SoftUni Buditel, grades 9–11. My students don't do textbook exercises; they work on live client briefs and present to the client at the end — [UWEAR](https://uwear.bg) (licensed children's merchandise) and Детска къща „Монтесори килимчета“.

### What I ran

**CEO, АЗТУЕС / TUES Alumni Association — 4 years and 10 months (2020–2025).**

- Grew the annual budget nearly **fourfold** (+299%) and the sponsor base from **17 to 54 IT companies** — funded entirely by private sponsorship, with no European projects and no state funding.
- Mentor and trainer of the student organizing teams of **Hack TUES** across **five editions** — it grew from 70 participants (2021) to **600+** (2025) and earned UNESCO recognition.

I also write about education and the skills young people actually need — [on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/skills-we-need-preparing-young-people-future-has-already-angelova-odiif) and at [Learning Without Boundaries](https://myteamhistory.wordpress.com/) — and I was a guest on the Bulgarian podcast [Свръхчовекът](https://www.thesuperhumanpodcast.net/episode/458-kirilka-angelova/).
